# Urblo admin quick guide

Open https://urblo.com.au/admin on a desktop computer. Words in bold are the buttons and fields you will see on screen. Press **Help** at the top of any admin page to open this guide.

## Sign in
Enter your email and password, then press **Sign in**. Forgotten it? Press **Forgot password?** and follow the email. Press **Sign out** when you finish.

## Save, Publish and Update live page
- **Save** keeps your changes private. The website does not change.
- **Publish** puts the page on the website. If it is greyed out, the note beside it says what is missing.
- **Update live page** appears on pages that are already live. It changes the website straight away, after you confirm.
- **Hide** takes a project off the website without deleting it.

## Change an image on a project page
1. Open **Projects**, search for the project and open it.
2. In **Page images and video**, press **Replace image**. Pick a picture, or press **Choose file**, write an **Image description** and press **Upload original**.
3. Press **Save**, check with **Open preview**, then press **Publish** and **Open live page**.

Stone photos: open **Stone Library**, open the stone, press **Add or upload image**, wait for **All changes saved**, then press **Publish**.

## Add or update a project and mark materials
1. In **Projects**, press **New project** or open an existing one, and fill in each section.
2. Under a picture, press **Mark materials on this image**, click on the stone, then choose it under **Material**.
3. Press **Save**, then **Publish**.

## Edit a product
Open **Products** and choose the product. Press **Save product** (not live yet) or **Update live page** (already live). **Publish product** puts a new product on the website. New pictures go into **Media** first with **Upload media**; then choose them as **Hero image**.

## Handle an enquiry or sample request
Each one is also emailed to the Urblo inbox. In **Leads**, open it and reply to the customer, then set **Status**, **Assigned owner** and **Internal notes** and press **Save workflow**.

## Update company addresses and contact details
In **Settings**, change **Primary email**, **Primary phone**, **Office address** or **Warehouse address**, then press **Save settings**. The website changes straight away. Update the capability statement PDF as well.

## Who to ask
Ask your Website owner or CMS manager for a login, for access to **Leads** or **Settings**, or when a button stays greyed out. Send a screenshot and the page address.
